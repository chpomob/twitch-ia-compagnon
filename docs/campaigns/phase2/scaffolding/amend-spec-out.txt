NOT ENCODED / CAVEATS
- Nothing in the ruling was left out. Two points are worth knowing:
  1. A "proposed action" is not a record kind the executor knows (no such field on
     ActionObservation); I kept the ruling's phrase as "any other record that is
     not an interruption (a proposed action)" so the intent is visible.
  2. `cause` and `played_ms` can only ride in the `error` mapping (a non-success
     ActionObservation carries no `result`; extra keys in `error` are accepted by
     `_normalised_error`). AC38/AC39 say `error.cause` / `error.played_ms`
     explicitly; R1's wording "with cause `playback` and the `played_ms` reached"
     was left untouched.
  3. An outside cancellation of the executor's task (`except CancelledError` in
     `_run_invocation`) never adopts a provider record today, by design; the
     ruling's `cancelled` case is therefore stated for a module-authored `cancelled`
     record (drain-driven, as AC23/AC35 already assume), not for that path.
- Caller-enumeration tables were not extended (not among the four required edits);
  the `core/actions.py` row there still describes only the part-validation site.
