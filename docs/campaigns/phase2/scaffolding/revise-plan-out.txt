## What I could not resolve / flags for the spec owner

- **AC41's exemption list vs. R3's own text.** The P1 resolution reuses R3's 1-s `skipped:deadline` reserve as
  the transcription request's bound. R3 mandates that 1 s ("under 1 s remains before the call deadline"), but
  AC41's letter exempts only "R3's `capture_too_long` admission check" from the "no expression subtracting a
  literal or a setting from the call deadline" rule — so a literal reading of AC41 would already flag R3's own
  `skipped:deadline` check regardless of this plan. The plan records the gate's reading (P21 step 4: both R3
  reserves exempt, neither moving the recorder's kill from `expiry`) and says so explicitly; if the spec owner
  wants AC41's text to match, that is a one-clause spec amendment I did not make (spec untouched, per the
  constraints). No alternative satisfies R3 and R10 together: a `success` stamped at `expiry` is discarded by
  R10, and an interruption record would lose the capture R3 requires.
- **`failed:stt_timed_out` for drain/cancellation abandonment.** R3's `transcription_status` set is closed, so
  a request abandoned by `drain()` or a `CancelledError` after storing reports `failed:stt_timed_out` ("no
  answer within the time the request was allowed"). It is the most honest member of the closed set; a
  dedicated status would need a spec amendment.
- Frontmatter `version: "1.1"` left as-is (it mirrors the spec version); the amendment is announced in the
  intro and the round-2 table instead.
